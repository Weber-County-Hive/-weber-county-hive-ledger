Questions the record raises

  1. Utahns for Balanced Government returned the Utah Bankers Association's $100,000 in full six weeks after receiving it. Neither filing gives a reason. Why was it returned?
  2. Gershon Barnett, filing from Monsey, New York, has given $454,500 to Utah candidates and committees since 2022, including $125,000 to Spencer Cox. What is his interest in Utah politics?
  3. Utahns for Balanced Government paid companies tied to its own listed officers: 24NINE (the email domain on its Executive Director's filing) and McCauley & Associates (its listed Treasurer's firm). What work did those payments cover?
  4. Zions Bancorporation PAC gave the Utah Democratic Party four times what it gave the Utah Republican Party. Who at the bank decides where the employees' payroll contributions go?

These are questions, not conclusions. Read the filings and decide what they mean.

Sources and method

  • Itemized Utah campaign-finance disclosures at disclosures.utah.gov: contribution searches by donor name and year, and full DEX (CSV) exports for PACs.
  • PAC and political issues committee registrations (Statements of Organization) at disclosures.utah.gov, for officer names and titles.
  • Utah Division of Corporations business-entity records, where cited on a card.
  • Names are listed as filed. Donors that file under several spellings are combined, and each card lists the spellings found.
  • This page describes what the filings show. It makes no claim about anyone's intent or about whether any law was broken.
