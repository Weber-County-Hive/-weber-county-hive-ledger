Who these PACs are

Every leadership and industry PAC that appears as a recipient on this page — what it is and who runs it, per each PAC's own official state filing.

UHRECUtah House Republican Election Committee
The Utah House Republican caucus's official leadership PAC. Per its Statement of Organization: Mike Schultz (Speaker of the House) and Casey Snider (Majority Leader) are Primary Officers; Candice Pierucci (Majority Whip) is an Officer. Kyle Palmer is CFO/Treasurer, with sole signature authority over disbursements.
URSCCUtah Republican Senate Campaign Committee
The Utah Senate Republican caucus's official leadership PAC. Per its Statement of Organization: J. Stuart Adams (Senate President) and Kirk Cullimore (State Senator) are Primary Officers. Kyle Palmer is CFO/Treasurer — the same treasurer who runs UHREC.
Adams Leadership PAC
Senate President J. Stuart Adams' personal leadership PAC — separate from URSCC, the caucus-wide committee he also co-leads. Filed from Lehi, UT.
House Speaker Leadership PAC
Speaker Mike Schultz's personal leadership PAC — separate from UHREC, the caucus-wide committee he also co-leads. Director: Mike Schultz; Chief of Staff: Abby Osborne; CFO/Treasurer: Kyle Palmer.
Governors Leadership PAC
A gubernatorial leadership PAC. Its officers haven't been pulled in this research.
Utah County Legislative PACUCL PAC
Created 3/9/2025 at the same Layton address as UHREC and URSCC. Per its Statement of Organization: President Stephanie Gricius (the sitting HD-50 representative) and Treasurer Kyle Palmer.
Utahns for Balanced Governmenta political issues committee (PIC), not a PAC
Created 10/10/2018 by Marty Carpenter, Gov. Gary Herbert's former campaign manager, to oppose Constitutional Amendment C. Per its registration: Marty Carpenter (Executive Director), Mike McCauley (Treasurer), Tyler Carpenter (Secretary / CFO-Treasurer of record), Brad Taylor (Advisor), Wesley Smith (Director). Its nonprofit filing was incorporated by Washington, DC attorney Matthew T. Sanderson. It took in $360,000 (2024-2025), returned $110,000, and paid firms including Irish Elk LLC, a company of UHREC treasurer Kyle Palmer.
Utah Bankers Association PAC
Represents the Utah Bankers Association. Per its Statement of Organization: President Howard M. Headlee (also CFO/Treasurer) and Senior Vice President Sara Matute. Funded largely by member banks.
Zions Bancorporation PAC
Per its Statement of Organization (created 12/22/2008): Chairman Jennifer Johnston, CFO/Treasurer Steven K. Earley, members Becky Kearns and Rob Brough. Funded by biweekly payroll contributions from bank employees.
Tourism Works PAC
The tourism industry's PAC, created 7/27/2012 in Draper and funded mainly by the Utah Tourism Industry Association. Per its Statement of Organization: Primary Officers Celina Sinclair (Executive Director) and Des Barker; Treasurer Lesha Coltharp; President Breck Dockstader; Vice President Chris Eggleton; Past-President Joan Hammer; plus a 13-member governing board. The filing lists addresses, not employers, for board members.
The Boeing Company PAC
The Utah arm of Boeing's corporate PAC, registered 10/13/2014 from Arlington, Virginia. Per its Statement of Organization: Chairman Jeff Shockey; Treasurer and Primary Officer Howard Goodloe Sutton; Assistant Treasurer Emmanuel Tormes; Custodian of Records Brian Mercer. Funded by transfers from Boeing's federal PAC.
The Home Depot PAC
The Utah arm of The Home Depot's corporate PAC, registered 1/7/2020 from Washington, D.C. Per its Statement of Organization: Chairperson Heather Kennedy; Treasurer and Primary Officer Isabel Janci (VP Investor Relations); Assistant Treasurer Dan Mutert (Sr Director, Merchandising Finance). Funded by transfers from The Home Depot's federal PAC.
Utah Chamber PAC
The Salt Lake Chamber's PAC, registered 3/1/2019 at the Chamber's address. Per its registration: President & CEO Derek Miller, COO Heidi Walker, CFO/Treasurer Jill Kirsling.
Worth noting: Kyle Palmer is listed as CFO/Treasurer of UHREC, URSCC and House Speaker Leadership PAC, three of the largest recipients on this page, per each PAC's own filing. Utahns for Balanced Government also paid his company, Irish Elk LLC, $25,000 in 2025.
Why totals on this page are hard to fully trace: most donors here give through more than one channel at once — a direct check to an individual legislator, plus separate checks to one or more of these leadership PACs, which then redistribute their own pooled money to many of the same legislators. A single donor's real influence on a specific recipient can arrive by both paths simultaneously, and those paths don't always show up connected in a single disclosures.utah.gov search. The totals on this page count each donor's own outbound giving once; they do not attempt to trace money a second time after it passes through a PAC.
How to read this list. Every figure comes from itemized state campaign-finance filings. Some donor totals cover their entire documented giving; others cover only what's been fully pulled so far (marked with a +). The recipient shown on the back of each tile is that donor's single largest documented recipient — not necessarily their only one. Conclusion & questions readers should ask

This page documents eleven donors that don't fit a single industry: Larry H. Miller Company, the Utah Bankers Association, Tourism Works PAC, The Boeing Company PAC, The Home Depot PAC, Utah Metal Works, the Utah Chamber PAC, Zions Bancorporation PAC, Gershon Barnett, Utahns for Balanced Government, and Beacon Metals. Two of them (the Bankers Association and Barnett) each gave Utahns for Balanced Government $100,000; the committee returned the Bankers Association's gift six weeks later. In September 2026 the Bankers Association and Zions Bancorporation PAC both gave to the same six legislative leaders within ten days.

Questions worth asking, based on what's documented here:

  • Utahns for Balanced Government returned the Utah Bankers Association's $100,000 in full on 10/17/2024, six weeks after receiving it. Neither filing gives a reason. Why was it returned?
  • Gershon Barnett, filing from Monsey, New York, has given $454,500 to Utah candidates and committees since 2022, including $125,000 to Spencer Cox. What is his interest in Utah politics?
  • Utahns for Balanced Government paid 24NINE (the email domain on its Executive Director's filing) and McCauley & Associates (its listed Treasurer's firm). What work did those payments cover?
  • Zions Bancorporation PAC gave the Utah Democratic Party four times what it gave the Utah Republican Party. Who decides where the employees' payroll contributions go?
  • On 9/24/2026 the Bankers Association gave $70,000 to seven legislators in one day; six of them had received Zions Bancorporation PAC checks on 9/14/2026. What was on those legislators' agendas that month?
  • On 4/24/2026 Southern Utah Tourism LLC gave Tourism Works PAC $30,000 with the note "Grand and San Juan County." Five days later the PAC gave $25,000 to four campaigns (Jamie Harvey, Sylvia Stubbs, Bill WinField, Mike McCurdy). Which races were those, and did each campaign report the money?
  • The Home Depot PAC gave Sean Reyes's campaign $10,000 (2020 and 2022), then gave Derek Brown for Attorney General $7,500 (2024-2025). Boeing's PAC gave Spencer Cox $5,000 a year for five years, then $10,000 to the Utah Inaugural Commission. Which state agencies or bills, if any, involved these companies in those years?

These are open questions the public record raises, not conclusions this page draws. Readers should look at the primary sources linked throughout and decide for themselves what the documented facts mean.
