Who these donors are

Five tech-sector donors — three companies, two PACs — found almost entirely through keyword searches that also caught dozens of unrelated companies sharing "tech" in their name.

Lumen Technologies Service Group
National telecom company (formerly CenturyLink), filed from Monroe, LA. Gives consistently every year, bipartisan — both GOP and Democratic leadership infrastructure in the same months.
Micron Technologyincl. predecessor IM Flash Technologies
Major national semiconductor company with a large Utah/Lehi manufacturing presence. Gives blanket, systematic small checks to nearly every sitting legislator each cycle — the same structural pattern already documented for Utah Petroleum Association PAC. Its 2018 giving is filed under a predecessor name, IM Flash Technologies, the Intel/Micron joint venture Micron fully acquired that year.
Dell Technologies Inc PAC
National computer-hardware company's own PAC, filed from Washington, DC. Only one year pulled so far.
Utah Tech LeadsUtah Tech Leads Association / Utah Tech Leads PAC
A real, active Utah tech-industry PAC (not a name-search false positive) — the association funds its own PAC, which then gives broadly across both parties. What the parent association actually represents hasn't been identified yet.
Utah Tech PACSilicon Slopes Commons PAC
The official PAC of Silicon Slopes Commons, Utah's best-known tech-industry nonprofit hub. President & CEO Clint Betts is Primary Officer; Director of Operations Elizabeth Converse is CFO/Treasurer. Confirmed inactive today, but was a real spender in 2017-2018, including $50,000 to its own lobbyist in a single year.
Two names sound alike but are different organizations: "Utah Tech Leads" (a Draper-based PAC, address 1318 E Bent Pine Cv / 350 E 400 S) and "Utah Tech PAC" (Silicon Slopes Commons' PAC, 2600 Executive Pkwy, Lehi) are NOT the same entity — confirmed by different registered addresses. Keep these two separate in any write-up.

Conclusion & questions readers should ask

This page documents five tech-sector donors — Lumen Technologies, Micron Technology (including its 2018 predecessor entity IM Flash Technologies), Dell Technologies PAC, Utah Tech Leads, and Utah Tech PAC (Silicon Slopes Commons' own PAC, now inactive). Micron/IM Flash stands out for scale and consistency — a systematic, blanket-coverage donor giving small checks to nearly every sitting legislator in both parties every cycle, the same structural pattern already documented for Utah Petroleum Association PAC on the Energy page. Utah Tech Leads shows the same association-funds-its-own-PAC structure already seen in Real Estate's RPAC/NWAOR relationship. This industry generated more excluded "search noise" than any other bucket on this site — dozens of unrelated companies and even individuals caught only because their name contains the string "tech."

Questions worth asking, based on what's documented here:

  • What does Utah Tech Leads Association actually represent, and who funds the association itself before it funds its own PAC?
  • Why did Silicon Slopes Commons' PAC go inactive, and when exactly did that happen?
  • Is there any connection between Micron's escalating Utah political giving and its Lehi manufacturing operations, tax incentives, or state permitting decisions?
  • Is "Utah Tech University" (flagged separately, not one of the five donors on this page) really the public university itself giving to political candidates and party committees, or a data-labeling issue?

These are open questions the public record raises, not conclusions this page draws. Readers should look at the primary sources linked throughout and decide for themselves what the documented facts mean.
