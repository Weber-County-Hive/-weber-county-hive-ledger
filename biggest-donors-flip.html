Who these people are

The same handful of names run nearly every PAC on this page. Here's each one, broken out.

Kyle Palmer
Consultant — owns Irish Elk LLC
Show documented connections
  • Treasurer/CFO: UHREC, URSCC, HSLPAC, NUPAC, Adams Leadership PAC, UCL PAC — six PACs simultaneously
  • Irish Elk LLC was paid by all six of those PACs — over $500,000 combined, every payment coded only as "Other" or "Fundraising Fees"
  • Former Director, Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity (2021–2024) — while also serving as NUPAC's treasurer
  • Former partner at Election Hive (2018–2019), before Matthew Lusty took it over solo
The single financial nexus this whole page keeps running into — if a PAC's money looks like it disappears into a vendor, check whether that vendor is Irish Elk.

Show documented connections

J. Stuart Adams
Utah Senate President — also Chair of MIDA (Military Installation Development Authority)
Show documented connections
  • URSCC — Primary Officer 1 (the Senate GOP's official leadership PAC)
  • Adams Leadership PAC — his own personal leadership PAC
  • Originally appointed to the MIDA board in 2008 by then-Gov. Jon Huntsman Jr.; has chaired the board since. MIDA's voting board unanimously approved the Stratos Project Area Plan on 4/24/2026 — the same authority Dan Hemmert and Lincoln Shurtz both currently lobby, per their own entries above
  • Public records show Adams Leadership PAC received a $135,000 donation within seven days of that April 2026 MIDA vote — a documented timing fact; no direct link between the vote and the donation has been established beyond the dates themselves
  • Adams Leadership PAC gave $10,000 to Utah Forward PAC in 2022, a local Weber County PAC tied to the Gibson family network documented elsewhere in this investigation
  • Lost his June 2026 Republican primary; reporting at the time noted he could still retain his MIDA oversight role (including the Deer Valley East Village project) despite the loss, since MIDA appointments aren't tied to legislative reelection

Matthew Lusty
Owns Election Hive LLC — also a registered lobbyist under a separate business name, "Beekeeper Consulting"
Show documented connections
  • Campaign-management vendor for Cox's campaign and multiple PACs — paid to process campaign money, not a donor giving money out
  • Election Hive pulled $84,304 from UHREC alone — over 3x what was previously known before a comprehensive multi-year pull
  • Worked alongside Kyle Palmer at Election Hive in 2018–2019, before Palmer left to run PACs directly
  • His own lobbyist registration began 1/17/2023 with a mundane early client mix (Central Wasatch Commission, Turo, a veterinary association) — but by 2025–2026 added American Promise and Election Reformers Network, both focused specifically on election policy/reform — years into already running the firm processing campaign money for Cox, Adams, and other Utah GOP leadership
Lincoln Shurtz
Owner and Principal Consultant, Legislative Insight Consulting, LLC — a registered Utah lobbying firm
Show documented connections
  • Called "One of the Most Influential Lobbyists on Capitol Hill" by the Salt Lake Tribune, per his firm's own site
  • Helped create MIDA itself and currently represents it as an active lobbying client (confirmed by the Salt Lake Tribune, 5/19/2026) — a second lobbyist, alongside Dan Hemmert, with a direct hand in both building and now working the authority central to the Stratos data-center story
  • His own firm, Legislative Insight Consulting, both gave Lincoln Hill PAC $42,500 and separately received $16,000 back from it — a closed loop of payments between his firm and a PAC he co-founded
  • Client list includes Aetna, Molina, Intermountain Healthcare, Northrop Grumman, General Motors, and Oracle — firm claims 10 Fortune 100 clients and $20 billion in Utah capital investment represented
  • Registered lobbyist for O'Leary Ventures Company — Kevin O'Leary's (Shark Tank) firm — confirmed by the Salt Lake Tribune (5/19/2026) alongside Casey Hill
  • Co-founder of Lincoln Hill Partners PAC; also runs his own separate individual PAC ("Lincoln Shurtz"), which funded Derek Brown $5,000 in 2024, the same year Brown was an officer of Lincoln Hill PAC
  • His law-and-lobbying firm, Lincoln Hill Partners, formed via a formal merger (10/4/2021) with a firm called Utah Policy Advocates, per the firm's own press release — four named principals: Shurtz, Casey Hill, Chase Everton, and Derek Brown (an attorney, ex-Sidley & Austin DC office)
  • Silicon Slopes Commons was his own lobbying client (12/31/2019–1/29/2021) — already tracked elsewhere on this page as a confirmed-complete $55,000 single donation to Slopes PAC, dated 12/1/2021, about 10 months after this lobbying relationship ended
  • The Utah Jazz are a current client (since 1/18/2024) — owned by Scott Ryan Smith, who separately and personally donated $80,000 to Lincoln Hill PAC, matching Fides LLC's $80,000 gift 12 days apart
  • Full registration confirms Utah Association of Counties as a long-running client (since 2014) and Creekstone Energy as an actual paying client (since 7/21/2026), not just a company he publicly cheered online
  • Testimonials on his firm's own site come from both Brad Wilson (then-Speaker) and Stuart Adams (Senate President) — both already central figures on this page
Two separate "Lincoln"-named PACs (Lincoln Hill Partners PAC and Lincoln Hill PAC) plus his own individual PAC all share overlapping recipients and a Holladay address cluster — treat as one coordinated network, not three unrelated committees.
Casey Hill
Registered lobbyist — co-founder of Lincoln Hill Partners/PAC alongside Shurtz and Derek Brown
Show documented connections
  • His own lobbying disclosure lists Election Hive as his own firm from 2019–2024 (67 months) — even though Election Hive's corporate registration names Matthew Lusty as manager. The exact relationship between the two isn't fully resolved, but Hill's own filings tie him directly to Election Hive, not just Lusty
  • SVP at EnergySolutions 2012–2019, then EnergySolutions became his continuous lobbying client since 2013 — a direct personal tie to a company already tracked on this page at $270,168
  • Registered lobbyist for O'Leary Ventures Company — Kevin O'Leary's (Shark Tank) firm — confirmed by the Salt Lake Tribune (5/19/2026), which also confirms Election Hive manages the campaigns of Adams, Cox, AND Derek Brown simultaneously
  • Utah Medical Association has been his client since 1/25/2019 — this donor was researched earlier in this project ($18,200 total, SenDemPAC as its top recipient) but isn't one of the top-10 companies currently shown on this page
  • 60+ lobbying clients on file including Salesforce, Netflix, Lenovo, Snap, Roblox, Expedia Group, Dakota Pacific Real Estate, Plain City, and Qualtrics (since January 2026 — see Scott Ryan Smith below)
  • Controls Gathering Inc., a signature-gathering company — documented payments totaling $150,000+ in 2024–2025
A shared phone line connects Election Hive, Qualtrics, O'Leary Ventures, and other Hill clients as registered lobbying contacts — worth treating as one coordinated office, not separate arrangements.
Derek Brown
Utah Attorney General — also a Lincoln Hill Partners named principal and Lincoln Hill PAC Director/Officer
Show documented connections
  • Named as one of four principals in Lincoln Hill Partners' own 10/4/2021 merger press release — an attorney who previously practiced government, regulatory, and administrative law at Sidley & Austin LLP's Washington D.C. office, providing legal services to the firm's clients
  • Co-founded Lincoln Hill PAC (created 4/27/2022) and remained listed as a Director/Officer even after being elected Attorney General — the state's chief law enforcement officer sitting as an officer of one of the most compliance-flagged PACs in this whole investigation

  • Documented 2024–2025 total received from this network: $294,000+ — including $114,000+ from Election Hive alone, both years labeled only "Other" or "Campaign Expense"
Election-law watchdogs would flag this as a structural conflict: he simultaneously serves as the state's top law-enforcement official and as an officer of the PAC he co-founded.
