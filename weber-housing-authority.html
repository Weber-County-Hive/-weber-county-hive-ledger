The Housing Authority Loop

A Weber County commissioner sits as trustee of the housing authority his own county funds and administers — alongside his wife, also a trustee — while the authority pays his family's real estate companies out of the same office building he works in.

The appointment, on the record

Weber County Commissioner Gage Froerer sits as a trustee of the Weber Housing Authority. His wife, Gloria Froerer, also sits as a trustee of the same authority, and separately directs its affiliated nonprofit, Friends of the Weber Housing Authority.

This wasn't discovered later — it was stated openly at the time of Froerer's appointment. Weber County Commission minutes from Sept. 17, 2019 (a meeting Froerer did not attend) record Commission Chair Scott Jenkins explaining the appointment to his colleagues:

Jenkins told the commission he and Commissioner Harvey asked Froerer to serve on the board because "it is in his portfolio," and stated there was no conflict in his wife also serving on it. Commissioner Harvey agreed. No further analysis or vote on the question is recorded in the minutes.

What the authority paid his companies

Weber County warrant reports from January 2023 through December 2025, reviewed individually, document $19,623 in total payments from the Weber Housing Authority to two Froerer family entities — RESCOM INC and Froerer Real Estate and Property Management — each categorized in the county's own records as "Weber Housing Auth Housing Payments."

One building, three connections

The Weber Housing Authority's own office is at 2380 Washington Blvd., Suite #112, Ogden — the same building that houses Froerer's Weber County Commissioner office, and the same building that housed Highlander NP Housing, a now-dissolved nonprofit Froerer directed.

On Dec. 23, 2025, the Weber County Commission approved a consent-agenda item funding construction on the Housing Authority's office space in that same building. Commissioner Harvey moved, Froerer seconded, and all three commissioners voted in favor.

Who processes the money

An interlocal agreement between the county and the authority establishes that Weber County itself provides all administrative and financial processing for the Weber Housing Authority. The same county government Froerer serves on as commissioner processes the payments the authority — where he and his wife both sit as trustees — makes to his family's businesses.

Also on the record: on Aug. 26, 2025, the commission approved a consent-agenda memorandum of understanding between the Sheriff's Office, the Housing Authority, and Weber Human Services on a supportive-housing program. Harvey moved, Froerer seconded and voted aye.

The disclosure gap

Froerer's own annual conflict-of-interest disclosure forms — filed with Weber County in January 2025 and January 2026 — list his board memberships on the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District and Central Weber Sewer District. Neither form lists the Weber Housing Authority trusteeship, despite it being active and despite the authority paying his family's companies during the exact period both forms cover.

This is the third confirmed board-seat omission on Froerer's recurring disclosure forms, following the same pattern already documented with his Military Installation Development Authority and Little Mountain Service Area roles elsewhere in this investigation.
