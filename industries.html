How to read this list. Each row is a total confirmed on that industry's own page — click through for the full donor-by-donor breakdown, year-by-year bars, top recipients, and "who runs it" details. A + means research is still in progress on that page; the number is a floor, not a ceiling. Industries are added to this list as their own pages get built — this is not yet every dollar tracked in this investigation, just what's been organized by industry so far.
These numbers will change. * Each total counts money once: when one donor on a page gave to another donor on the same page, that money is subtracted. 2026 figures run through the latest filed reports; we are waiting on final year-end reports. 38 donors/PACs have been researched and organized into industries so far (Real Estate 9, Energy 10, Everything Else 11, Tech 8) — that is nowhere close to every company or PAC giving money in Utah politics. Some entities here are marked complete; most are marked + because not every year, every report page, or every name-spelling variant has been pulled yet. Treat every total on this page as a floor, not a final count, and expect it to keep growing as more PACs are checked.
