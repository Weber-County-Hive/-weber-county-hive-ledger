How to read this list. Each row is a total confirmed on that industry's own page — click through for the full donor-by-donor breakdown, recipient flow charts, and "who runs it" details. A + means research is still in progress on that page; the number is a floor, not a ceiling. Industries are added to this list as their own pages get built — this is not yet every dollar tracked in this investigation, just what's been organized by industry so far.
These numbers will change. 29 donors/PACs have been researched and organized into industries so far (Real Estate 5, Energy 10, Everything Else 6, Tech 8) — that is nowhere close to every company or PAC giving money in Utah politics. Some entities here are marked complete; most are marked + because not every year, every report page, or every name-spelling variant has been pulled yet. Treat every total on this page as a floor, not a final count, and expect it to keep growing as more PACs are checked.
