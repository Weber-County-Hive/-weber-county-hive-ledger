01Who runs these PACs

Names below come directly from each committee's own registration or campaign-finance filing — not inferred. Where we don't have a confirmed officer on file, it's marked as such rather than guessed.

Utah House Republican Election Committee (UHREC)
Mike Schultz (Speaker) and Casey Snider (Majority Leader) — Primary Officers. Candice Pierucci (Majority Whip) — Officer. Kyle Palmer — CFO/Treasurer AND Director, sole signature authority over disbursements.
House Speaker Leadership PAC
Mike Schultz — Director. Kyle Palmer (Irish Elk LLC) — paid administration/fundraising consultant, same role he holds at NUPAC and Utah House Republican Election Committee.
Adams Leadership PAC
Named for and controlled by Sen. J. Stuart Adams (Senate President), Primary Officer. Kyle Palmer — also a listed Primary Officer AND Chief Financial Officer/Treasurer, same dual role he holds across multiple other PACs in this network.
Utah Republican Senate Campaign Committee (URSCC)
Sen. J. Stuart Adams (Senate President) and Sen. Kirk Cullimore — Primary Officers. Kyle Palmer — CFO/Treasurer, same role he holds at other PACs on this page. Represents "Members of the Utah Senate Republican Caucus." Irish Elk LLC (Palmer's firm) is URSCC's single largest expenditure recipient — $326,282.55 since 2022.
The pattern across all six
Kyle Palmer holds Treasurer and/or CFO authority over five of the six PACs on this page — UHREC, House Speaker Leadership PAC, Adams Leadership PAC, URSCC, and NUPAC. He's also Director of two of them. The one PAC he's not an officer of, Utah Petroleum Association PAC, is instead run directly by Chevron's and Marathon Petroleum's own government-affairs executives.
Utah Petroleum Association PAC
Chairman Brad Shafer — Sr. Manager Government & Public Relations, Marathon Petroleum. Vice Chairman Chad Calvert — General Manager Government Affairs, Chevron. Secretary/Treasurer Rikki Hrenko-Browning — President of the Utah Petroleum Association itself. The board is staffed directly by two major oil companies' own government-affairs executives.
Northern Utah Legislative PAC (NUPAC)
Sen. F. Ann Millner and Rep. Casey Snider — Primary Officers. Kyle Palmer — Treasurer/CFO (Irish Elk LLC).

03Confirmed direct contributions — Big West Oil & rPlus Energies

confirmed — direct disclosure data

Unlike the candidate chains on the main Ledger page, every figure here is a traced, confirmed transaction pulled directly from disclosures.utah.gov for these two companies specifically — not a combinatorial pairing. This data was researched separately from the original four source files and is not included in the KPI totals at the top of this page.

Big West Oil — where the money went (2023-2026, $80,120 total)

rPlus Energies LLC — 2026 recipients ($4,600 total)

Big West Oil — a North Salt Lake refinery (333 W Center Street). Four straight years of giving to Gov. Spencer Cox, landing on an identical $7,500/year amount for 2024-2026. Adams Leadership PAC ($1,000/year, 2023-2025) and J. Stuart Adams personally ($1,000/year, 2025-2026) both show the same repeating-amount pattern. Almost no giving to individual rank-and-file legislators after 2023 — a genuinely different strategy from the blanket small-check approach used by Utah Petroleum Association PAC.

rPlus Energies LLC — developer/owner of the Green River Energy Center, an 800MW solar-plus-battery-storage project in Emery County (~$1.1B in construction financing). Gave $800-$1,000 each to five legislators within a five-week window in mid-2026: Derrin Owens, Colin Jack, Todd Weiler, Wayne Harper, and Mike McKell — all five already appear elsewhere in this investigation's other files.

05Sourcing, corrections and research status

Primary source: Utah Lieutenant Governor's Office campaign finance disclosure database, disclosures.utah.gov. All figures are itemized contributions and expenditures pulled directly from that system — via its Advanced Search "Download Data by Year" tool and via DEX CSV exports per committee — across multiple research sessions.

Coverage: spans 2020–2026 depending on the entity; coverage year-by-year varies by filer and is noted per entity in the underlying research files. Several years are confirmed partial (multi-page disclosures.utah.gov results where not every page was captured) — every total on this page is a floor, not a certified complete-year figure, until reconciled against the full filing.

Known data-cleaning step: "Energy Solutions" and "EnergySolutions" — two spellings for the same company appearing inconsistently across filings — have been merged into a single entity here. The Larry H. Miller Company donor-side spelling variants have also been merged. Additional confirmed data for Big West Oil and rPlus Energies LLC (Section 03 below) come from a separate, independently-researched dataset and are not included in the totals on the main Ledger page.

Correction, 8/13/26: Irish Elk LLC's total (Kyle Palmer, the recurring PAC-administration/fundraising vendor) was undercounted at $400,732.55 — that figure only combined his UHREC ($74,450) and URSCC ($326,282.55) payments. Cross-checking against the Operation Gigawatt research tracker confirms two more PACs paying him: Adams Leadership PAC (~$114,100, approximate — not yet itemized to the cent) and House Speaker Leadership PAC ($65,975). Corrected total across all four: $580,807.55.

Excluded from totals: transactions coded [DELETED] in the source filings — amount zeroed to $0.00 with no date preserved by the filer — are excluded here. Their existence (in some committees' filings, dozens per year, every year) is itself documented separately and is not reflected in any dollar figure on this page.
