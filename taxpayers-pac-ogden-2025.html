Taxpayers PAC and the 2025 Ogden Council Races

A Layton PAC formed in July 2025 reported $16,600 in contributions: $15,000 from one Phoenix, Ariz., donor and $1,600 from the Weber County Republican Party. It then reported $15,600 to four Ogden City Council candidates. Three returned the money. One returned most of it.

01What this PAC is

"Taxpayers PAC" registered with the state on July 17, 2025. Its Statement of Organization lists an address of 721 Main St #327 in Layton. [1]

Zach Lautenschlager is listed as President and as CFO, with the occupation "Consultant." Nathan Barnes is listed as Vice President, with the occupation "Business Consultant." [1]

The name is similar to other groups, but this is a separate committee. We found no record linking it to Taxpayer Shield PAC (the Amendment B campaign) or to the Utah Taxpayers Association. [1]

KSL.com called it "Taxpayer PAC" in its November 2025 story. The state registration says "Taxpayers PAC." [1][2]

06What the PAC's filing does not show yet

The returned money. KSL.com reported four returns, about $14,100 in all. [2] The PAC's export we read lists only the two contributions above as money coming in. It does not list the returned money as a refund or contribution. [1] Returns may be filed later or in a different way. Readers can check the PAC's newest reports.

2026. The export showed no 2026 activity as of Oct. 7, 2026. [1]

07Questions to ask, and whom to ask

The Lieutenant Governor's elections office: when a candidate returns a PAC's contribution, where should the PAC show that money coming back?

Ogden City Recorder: where can the public read the city's $1,500 contribution limit and the candidates' own 2025 finance reports?

Weber County Republican Party: what the $1,600 to Taxpayers PAC on Oct. 23, 2025, was for, as shown in the party's own report.
